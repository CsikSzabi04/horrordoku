Case file #001 · very easy

Midnight Mass

Midnight. The bell of Saint Bartholomew’s Chapel rang by itself. Ábel, the Order’s scribe, was found at the foot of the belfry, a black raven feather in hand. Five people were in the chapel that night. Who killed Ábel?

  1. Pick a suspect from the cards.
  2. Place them on the grid — only one character per row and column.
  3. Every clue must hold. “Next to” = directly adjacent, in the same room.
  4. When everyone is in place: Submit. And pray you’re right…

Co-op: Killer

Someone among you is lying.

The server secretly picks a killer. They investigate with you and see the same board — but hinder you with three silent abilities. Nobody gets a notification. All that’s left for the others is observation, logic and the Meeting.

⏱️Time Warp

12:40

✍️Text Swap

Vlad sat on a bench in the Sacristy.
notification: —

🕳️Vanish

An object disappears — and the cell looks free. Place anything there, and when it returns, everything is lost and the cell is locked forever.

cooldown: 60 s · 8 s effect

Counterplay: 🔎 Sniff — “did anyone use an ability in the last 30 s?” · 🗳️ Meeting — discussion, then a vote: guess right and you expose them. Guess wrong… and they get the last laugh.

Power rules

Species matter.

A character’s species changes where they can stand — and how to read their clue. The rules are part of the puzzle.

🧛Vampire

Can’t stand on a moonlit cell, nor next to a mirror.

👻Ghost

“Next to” holds through walls for them, and they can stand on occupied objects.

😈Demon

One clue lies. The question is: which one?

🐺Werewolf

On a full moon they can only be outdoors — and the Moon is “up” right now.

🧙Witch

Only the witch may stand on pentagram cells. Everyone else avoids them.

🧟Undead

Can’t be adjacent to a living person — not even diagonally. They can smell them.

🕷️Shapeshifter

Has two cards. You have to work out which one is real.
